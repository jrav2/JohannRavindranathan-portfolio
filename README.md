## EV Charger Mapping
This group project was a showcase for our time spent learning about digital geographies. This was my introduction to web programming with HTML/CSS/JS. We had spent time making webpages and maps that could be hosted online. <br>

For our project, we chose to create a website that helped owners of electric vehicles (BEV / PHEV) find charging infrastructure across Washington state. We pulled data through the Washington Geospatial Open Data Portal, and obtained spatial data from 2024. From there we set about visualizing the data to help drivers assess the availablility of chargers in areas of interest.<br>

## Report on recreation site closures by WA Department of Natural Resources
This was a capstone project completed for the Washington DNR.<br>

My teammates and I were tasked with conducting analysis on how DNR recreation site closures would affect local residents and Washingtonians. After completing the initial investigatory and research phases, we began to input data into ArcGIS Pro and run our individual analyses. I was tasked with looking into environmental and equity considerations. <br>

The files included were the two progress updates we presented, the final presentation of our findings, and the written report we submitted to our sponsor at the end of the project.
