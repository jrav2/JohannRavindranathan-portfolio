## EV Charger Mapping
This group project was a showcase for my time spent with Web GIS, online interactive maps, and web programming with HTML/CSS/JS.<br>

We created a proof-of-concept website that helps owners of electric vehicles (BEV / PHEV) find charging infrastructure across Washington state. Spatial data for charging lots and infrastructure, accurate through 2024, was pulled through the Washington Geospatial Open Data Portal. This was then visualized into different map styles, to offer drivers multiple perspectives on charger availability. I worked on styling and integrating our basemap, developed a heat map for the spread of locations across WA, and further configuration and development of our website. br>

## Report on recreation site closures by WA Department of Natural Resources
This was a capstone project completed for the Washington DNR.<br>

My teammates and I were tasked with analyzing how scheduled DNR recreation site closures (announced in late 2025, officially enacted in 2026) would affect Washington residents. After completing the initial investigatory and research phases, we each worked with the data in ArcGIS Pro to explore different possible effects. I was tasked with looking into environmental and equity considerations. <br>

The files included are the two progress updates we presented, the final presentation of our findings, and the written report we submitted to our sponsor at the end of the project.
