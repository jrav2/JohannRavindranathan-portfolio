Movie Theaters in the Greater Seattle Area

This webmap is a proof of concept. It displays movie theaters in the vicinity of the Greater Seattle area, centered roughly around the UW Seattle campus.

Points are 17 theaters in the vicinity of Seattle.
Not an exhaustive list, just the first batch that came when I searched.

The polygons are simulating buffers. These could display the distance from campus. This would indicate levels of accessibility. There is a larger and a smaller polygon, representing two levels of distance.

To provide some sense of spatial reference, I-5 and I-90 have been labeled on the map.

The basemap is the Thunderforest - Atlas map. It is a clean and uncluttered option, which helps with navigation and readability.

This map is useful for identifying local movie theaters and their quality of service. Further expansion could add the price of tickets, scheduled movies, travel times, and perhaps the size of the rooms.
