# Effect of Recreation Site Closures
For my capstone project, my teammates and I were assigned to a project sponsored by WA Dept. of Natural Resources. 

### Mission Brief
In late 2025, WA DNR announced that several sites would be closing due to budget cuts for the agency and overextended resources. At the time, the full lists of sites had not been finalized. DNR was interested in learning what effect these closures would have on residents and users of the facilities. Since DNR didn't have direct data for this, this was an opportunity for us.
<br>

Using the skills and intuition we had developed by this point, we created a plan to study the effects of the recreation site closures. <br>

We analyzed the loss of accessibility for some of the most common hobbies and outdoor pastimes, the demographics of individuals likely to be affected by the closures, and considered ways to describe the change to quality of life.
